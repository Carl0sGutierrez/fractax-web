Under the hood: how Fractax keeps each certificate apart

What Fractax does so that each certificate stays on its own, never mixed with the others.

A separate space for each certificate

Each identity has its own cookies, cache, storage and sessions. What one identity does on a site, no other identity sees.

Certificates never cross over

A regular browser remembers the certificate you picked for the whole browser, which is why it keeps using the first one. In Fractax, when a site asks for a certificate, only the one for the identity you are in is used.

Sessions kept in memory only

Site sessions are kept in memory, not on disk: when you close Fractax they are gone. “Log out” clears that identity’s session and starts a clean one, so the site asks for the certificate again.

Sites isolated from Fractax

Every page runs isolated, with no access to your files or to Fractax. Camera, microphone, location and browser notifications are blocked: only copying to the clipboard, full screen and opening AutoFirma are allowed.

Security

Fractax works with digital certificates, so security is not an add-on: it is built into how every piece of data is stored.

A vault encrypted with AES-256

Everything Fractax stores is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is derived from your master password with scrypt and is never stored anywhere: without the password, the vault cannot be opened.

The private key stays in your system

If you import a certificate from the Mac Keychain or the Windows certificate store, Fractax does not copy the private key: it only keeps its details and fingerprint to recognise it, and signing in to sites still goes through the operating system. Only if you turn on automatic notifications does it keep a copy, encrypted inside the vault, to check DEHú.

An app that can’t be tampered with

On Mac, Fractax is signed and notarised by Apple. The package checks its own integrity: if anyone changes its code, it won’t start. The installed version has no debugging modes that would let other code run.

No tracking

Fractax sends no usage statistics and does not record what you do. Diagnostic logs are off, and if you turn them on they hide ID numbers (DNI, NIE and CIF).

Your certificates never leave your computer

Fractax has no servers where certificates are stored. There is no cloud to set up or to secure: the risk of uploading sensitive data to the internet goes away, because that step doesn't exist.

  • Everything Fractax stores is encrypted on your computer, in a vault protected by your master password.
  • If you import a certificate from the system store (Keychain on Mac, Windows certificate store), the private key is not copied: it stays where it was.
  • Government websites and DEHú are accessed from your own computer, just like when you log in yourself.
  • Documents from the notifications you open are saved only on your computer.

Getting started

In a few minutes, with no sign-up and no credit card.

  1. Download Fractax

    For Mac, Windows and Linux. It installs like any other app.

  2. Create your master password

    It protects the encrypted vault where Fractax keeps everything on your computer.

  3. Add your certificates

    From a .pfx or .p12 file, or from the ones already installed on your system.

  4. Work with all of them at once

    Pick a certificate in the sidebar and go to the websites. Switch to another with one click.

Your notifications, without checking DEHú one by one

Fractax checks DEHú for you across all your certificates and shows what's pending and how many days each notification has left.

  • Notifications and communications for each certificate, in one tab.
  • Open them in Fractax, always after you confirm.
  • Document, attachments and receipt saved on your computer.
  • Turns itself on: click the bell and Fractax prepares the certificate, checks the DEHú registration and shows your notifications, with a green tick at each step.

DEHú requires each certificate to be registered once as a Large Recipient (Gran Destinatario). If it already is, there's nothing to do. If not, Fractax tells you and guides you: it's free. If you're an adviser or lawyer and your clients have authorised you, your certificate also checks their notifications.

What Fractax doesn't do yet

So you know what to expect before installing it.

  • You can't see a notification's content without opening it.
  • You can't reject notifications from Fractax.
  • Some bodies have their own mailbox and don't publish in DEHú. Their notifications don't appear in Fractax.
